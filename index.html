🌾 Udyogini – Empowering Women, Reviving Traditions

Udyogini, meaning “a female entrepreneur,” is a thoughtful initiative by the Sharvari Wasnik Foundation under its women empowerment wing.

It is built on a simple yet powerful idea — enabling women to become financially independent while preserving the richness of local traditions.

The initiative has begun with the production of nutritious atta made from:

  • 🌽 Makka (Maize)
  • 🌾 Bajra (Pearl Millet)
  • 🌿 Jowar (Sorghum)

These grains promote healthier eating habits rooted in Indian culture.

Future plans include:

  • Homemade achaar
  • Papads
  • Traditional Vidarbha delicacies
  • Stitching & tailoring training

The initiative focuses on women from Nandanwan slum areas, helping them achieve financial independence, dignity, and confidence.

📱 Urban Sakhi App

A platform connecting women with part-time work like cooking, cleaning, ironing, and stitching services.

🍽 Cooking Suggestions

🌾 Bajra Bhakri

  • Use warm water
  • Flatten by hand
  • Cook on hot tawa
  • Serve with ghee or chutney

🌽 Makka Roti

  • Knead gently with warm water
  • Shape by hand
  • Cook on medium heat
  • Best with sarson ka saag

🌿 Jowar Bhakri

  • Use hot water
  • Roll thin
  • Cook on tawa
  • Serve with dal or chutney

💚 Why These Grains Matter

Rich in fiber, minerals, and nutrients — they improve digestion, sustain energy, and are healthier than refined grains.
